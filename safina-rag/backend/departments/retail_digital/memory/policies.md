# NCBA Retail Digital Lending Policies

## Customer Vintage Policy

All customers must maintain an active banking relationship with NCBA for a minimum of 6 months before becoming eligible for digital lending products. This requirement ensures the bank has sufficient transaction history to assess creditworthiness and payment behavior.

## Days Past Due (DPD) Arrears Policy

Customers with loan arrears exceeding 3 days within the past 60 days are automatically excluded from digital lending eligibility. This lookback period of 60 days ensures recent payment discipline is maintained.

### Cooling Period After Arrears Clearance
Upon clearing all outstanding arrears, customers must observe a mandatory 60-day cooling period before re-qualifying for new loans. This policy allows the bank to verify sustained positive payment behavior.

## Account Ownership Policy

Only accounts with single ownership and sole signatory mandates qualify for digital lending products. Joint accounts, accounts with multiple signatories, or accounts requiring dual authorization are excluded. This ensures clear accountability and simplifies loan recovery processes.

## Mobile Banking Requirement

All loan applicants must have an active NCBA mobile banking profile (ELMA). This requirement enables:
- Digital loan application and disbursement
- Real-time account monitoring
- Automated repayment processing
- Customer self-service capabilities

Inactive mobile banking profiles result in automatic exclusion from digital lending eligibility.

## Risk Classification Policy

### Internal Risk Classification
Customers must maintain an internal risk classification of A5 or higher. The internal rating considers:
- Account turnover patterns
- Balance maintenance
- Relationship depth
- Transaction regularity

### CRB Risk Classification  
A minimum Credit Reference Bureau (CRB) risk classification of A5 is mandatory. Lower classifications indicate higher default probability and result in automatic exclusion.

## Turnover and Recency Requirements

### Credit Turnover Consistency
Customers must demonstrate consistent credit turnovers for at least 6 consecutive months. Erratic or declining turnover patterns indicate financial instability and affect eligibility.

### Recency of Transactions
Regular account activity is required. Accounts with irregular or dormant transaction patterns may be flagged as inactive and excluded from lending.

## Affordability Assessment

The bank conducts affordability checks to ensure the Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) does not exceed the customer's estimated income based on account turnover. Loans that would overburden customers are declined to promote responsible lending.

## Special Categories

### Staff Accounts
Internal bank staff members follow separate lending policies and are excluded from standard digital lending products to prevent conflicts of interest.

### USAID-Affiliated Accounts
Accounts affiliated with USAID programs follow different lending arrangements and are excluded from standard digital lending eligibility.

### Scheme Transfers
Customers who have transferred loans to or from other financial institutions under restructuring schemes are excluded until the scheme arrangements are completed.

## Linked Customer Base Policy

For customers with linked accounts or relationships (such as business and personal accounts), all linked entities must maintain positive risk ratings. Negative ratings on any linked account affect eligibility across all accounts.

## Active Account Status

Only active accounts qualify for digital lending. Accounts marked as inactive, dormant, closed, or under any restriction are automatically excluded.

## Review and Appeals

Customers who believe they have been incorrectly excluded may request a manual review through:
- NCBA Contact Center
- Visit to any branch
- Written appeal to customercare@ncbagroup.com

Reviews are completed within 5 business days. Decisions are communicated via SMS and email.

## Policy Updates

These policies are reviewed quarterly and may be updated based on:
- Regulatory changes
- Market conditions
- Risk assessment outcomes
- Customer feedback

Customers are notified of material policy changes through mobile banking notifications and SMS alerts.
